Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Orbit Loop is a personal tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel @OrbitLoopSim to upload and schedule videos to that channel. It is not offered to the public.

What data the app accesses

The app requests one Google permission: YouTube upload (youtube.upload). It is used only to upload videos, with their titles, descriptions and publish times, to the owner's own channel.

How data is stored

The sign-in token is saved only on the owner's own computer. The app does not read, collect or store any other Google account data, and does not use any analytics or cookies.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared with or transferred to anyone. The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time from myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions can be sent through the YouTube channel @OrbitLoopSim.
